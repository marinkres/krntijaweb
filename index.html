What it does

The sound of a door that doesn't open

Every mix ends up in a car sooner or later. KRNTIJA skips the part where you have to go outside, it runs your signal through the preamp, the paper cone and the rattling metal box of six cars that have done their kilometres.

It isn't a reverb and it isn't a bitcrusher. Each preset is a measured frequency response plus everything that comes with it: preamp saturation, clipping when the low end gets pushed, a slow wobble on the top, and a cabinet resonance that lets nothing through below its own limit.

Controls

Owner's handbook

Four knobs, one switch and a row of buttons. Nothing is hidden in a menu, because the original didn't have menus either.

Car buttonsSix buttons, bottom row

Picks the speaker and the character of the distortion. Yugo and Fico are the filthiest of the six; Merc and E36 stay closest to clean. Switching cars changes everything downstream, so start here.

GasKnob

How hard you push into the distortion. Turn it up and the signal gets more aggressive and starts to break up.

MileageKnob

How wrecked the car is. Higher readings lose the top end and make the plastic rattle harder. The same speaker, twenty years further along.

MixKnob

Balance between the processed and the dry signal. At 100% everything goes through the car.

VolumeKnob

Output level. The needle dances along with it as a VU meter.

KRC switchToggle, under the gauges

Turns the bad-contact crackle on and off. Off by default. The display prints CRACKLE ON or CRACKLE OFF when you flip it.

Installing

There is no installer

Windows

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Copy Krntija.vst3 into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
  3. Start your DAW and rescan plugins.

macOS

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. VST3 goes into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3, AU into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  3. If Gatekeeper complains, right-click the file and choose Open.

Your DAW can't find it

  1. Check that your DAW is 64-bit — 32-bit hosts aren't supported.
  2. Clear the plugin cache in your DAW's preferences and rescan.
  3. Still nothing? Email below and say which DAW and which system.
